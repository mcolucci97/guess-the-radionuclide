# RRCI expert review form

For each candidate item, experts should receive the item **without the key visually emphasized first**, then reveal the proposed key/rationale.

Rate 1–4:

1. **Scientific accuracy**
   - 1 = incorrect / materially misleading
   - 2 = major scientific revision needed
   - 3 = accurate with minor nuance needed
   - 4 = accurate as written

2. **Relevance to concept**
   - 1 = not relevant
   - 2 = weakly relevant
   - 3 = relevant
   - 4 = highly relevant

3. **Clarity**
   - 1 = unclear
   - 2 = major rewrite
   - 3 = minor edit
   - 4 = clear

4. **Explorer/Scientist appropriateness**
   - 1 = wrong level
   - 2 = substantial mismatch
   - 3 = acceptable
   - 4 = well targeted

5. **Distractor diagnosticity**
   - 1 = implausible/trivial
   - 2 = weak
   - 3 = plausible
   - 4 = maps clearly to a meaningful misconception/reasoning error

6. **Ambiguity / multiple defensible answers**
   - yes / no
   - if yes, explain.

Free comments:
- scientific nuance;
- wording;
- missing misconception;
- suggested alternative distractor;
- recommendation: retain / revise / remove.

## Panel-level questions

After item review ask:
- Is any essential construct missing?
- Is any construct overrepresented?
- Is the test overly medical relative to the stated broader radioactivity objective?
- Are risk statements neutral and scientifically contextual rather than persuasive?
- Are Explorer items understandable at age ~14 without prior nuclear physics?
- Does the Scientist extension genuinely require deeper reasoning rather than trivia?
