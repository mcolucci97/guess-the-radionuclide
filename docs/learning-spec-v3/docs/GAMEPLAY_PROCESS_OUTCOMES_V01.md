# Gameplay process outcomes V0.1

These outcomes are intentionally separated from the RRCI pre/post test.

## Primary process measures

For each reliably parsed yes/no question:
- candidate count before question;
- yes/no partition sizes;
- binary entropy / expected information gain;
- actual candidate reduction after answer;
- concept(s) invoked;
- whether the question was spontaneous or suggested;
- assistance level.

Session summaries:
- median information value per independent question;
- proportion of strongly discriminating questions;
- candidate reduction per turn;
- diversity of scientific concepts used in questions;
- change from early to late turns;
- number of questions requiring full suggestion vs faded hint vs no hint.

## Why it is separate

A player can become strategically better at the deduction game without gaining radioactivity knowledge. Conversely, a scientifically meaningful question may be strategically unbalanced. Therefore gameplay strategy and conceptual learning must not be collapsed into one score.

## Calibration items

G01 (balanced 10/10 vs 19/1 split) and G02 (yes/no format) may be used in tutorials or pilot checks, but are not part of the RRCI score. Prefer direct behavioral evidence from gameplay.
